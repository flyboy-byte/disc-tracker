Disc Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026 · Last updated: August 19, 2026

Disc Tracker (the “app”) is a local-first disc golf bag tracker for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: everything stays on your device, and the one optional network feature is off unless you turn it on.

Open source

Disc Tracker is free and open source software, released under the GPLv3 license. The complete source code is published at github.com/flyboy-byte/disc-tracker. You can audit every line yourself to verify the claims in this policy. Issues and contributions are welcome via the repository.

Data we collect

None. Disc Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or otherwise send any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics anywhere. The app has no user accounts and no servers of its own it talks to.

Data stored on your device

  • Your bag. Discs, flight numbers, plastic/weight/color/wear notes, today’s-bag selections, custom scorecards, and app preferences are saved locally on your device in a SQLite database. This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly export or back it up yourself (see below).
  • Backups & CSV export. The Backup & Restore and CSV export features write a file and hand it to Android’s share sheet — where that file goes from there (email, cloud drive, local save) is entirely your choice. The app itself never uploads it anywhere.
  • Disc Suggest preferences. Swiping a disc away in Disc Suggest saves a per-scenario reorder, and, in Buy mode, a small locally-derived preference profile (which flight numbers and manufacturers you’ve been passing on, used only to re-rank future suggestions). This never leaves your device, is never transmitted or analyzed anywhere, and can be reset anytime in-app (“Reset order” and the Learning on/off toggle on the Disc Suggest screen).

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. You can also clear it manually in Android Settings → Apps → Disc Tracker → Storage → Clear Data, or use the in-app “Delete all discs” option in Settings.

The one optional network feature

Disc Suggest and the Bag view can show a real measured flight-path reference image for a disc, sourced from the third-party Marshall Street database via discit-api.fly.dev. This is off by default — it only runs if you enable “Marshall Street reference images” in Settings. When enabled, the app sends the manufacturer and mold name of a disc you’re viewing to that API to fetch its image; no other personal or bag data is included in that request, and it only happens for a disc you’re actively looking at. Turning the setting off stops all requests to this API immediately.

Permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET — required only for the optional Marshall Street reference-image lookup above.
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below only) — used for CSV and backup file export/import through the share sheet and document picker.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — used for tap/haptic feedback on interactive controls.

Third parties

Disc Tracker does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs of any kind. The only third-party service the app ever talks to is the opt-in Marshall Street image lookup described above.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
